# What's new

## 2026-10-20

- New "Simple words" switch under the gear button. Turn it on and Claude writes its messages on the cards in easy words, with no hard grown-up terms. The Summary of a finished card uses easy words too.

## 2026-10-19

- The tree of Claude's choices now also shows at the top of the Summary tab, so you can see what Claude picked without opening the Conversation tab.
- The tree of Claude's choices now has a line around it, so you can see where it starts and ends. Click the purple "Decisions" button above it, which shows how many choices there are, to fold the tree away or open it again with a smooth slide. It stays the way you left it.
- When a choice in the tree has too many words to fit in the tree's strip, the big copy you see when you point at it now spreads over the whole screen, so you can read every word.

## 2026-10-18

- Claude's choices on a task now show as one tree that goes from left to right at the top of the Conversation tab. Each new choice grows from the one picked before it, and the tree grows on its own as Claude decides.
- The tree of Claude's choices now stays at the top of the Conversation tab while you scroll down through the messages. It is smaller now, and a choice grows bigger when you point at it, getting wider when needed so all its words show inside the tree.

## 2026-10-17

- Claude can no longer move your main project folder to another version of the work. Before, a task you said "go ahead" on could do that, and then joining other tasks' work into it got stuck. If it is already on another version, Claude can still move it back to the main one.
- In the list, a card now has the "New task from this" button and the check that says if a new task is a good idea.

## 2026-10-15

- The top of the board now says "OneMoreTask" and its version number, which goes up each time a new version comes out. The Update button shows which version you will get.
- When a newer Claude Code is out, a button at the top tells you. Click it, say yes, and the board updates Claude Code for you.
- When Claude has questions for you, they now always show on the card with a button for each answer. Before, Claude sometimes asked in a hidden window, and the card only said it was waiting.
- When Claude asks if it may do something, the "Allow" button on the card works again. Before, it sometimes said "the prompt has no such choice".
- A task you send to Claude now goes to the top of the next column, not the bottom. And you can scroll a long column again while Claude works: it no longer stops your scrolling every few seconds.
- The Update button now works away from home too, like at a friend's or in a café. Before, some places stopped it and it said the update failed.
- "Create repo" now works on very big folders. Before, it gave up after a minute and then said it could not create the repo. It now waits and tells you it can take a few minutes. It also shows the biggest folders with their size, so you can tick the ones to leave out.
- When Claude asks you several things at once, you now click one answer for each, then press "Send answers". No more typing letters like "1a 2b".
- Notes that pop up now make a little sound, also in Chrome and Edge. You can turn the sound off under View, "Notification sound".
- Pressing Send twice by mistake no longer starts two helpers on the same task.
- A reply that starts with a list ("- fix this") now reaches Claude. Before, it failed.
- Claude can no longer post an empty update on a task, or one that is just a dash.
- If a task's saved messages get damaged, they are kept aside instead of being wiped out by the next message.
- Tasks keep updating even when Claude's chat history folder is missing.
- If sending a task fails halfway, Claude no longer keeps working on it out of sight.
- If another app already uses the board's address, the board tells you so instead of opening that app.
- If Claude saved work in a side place the task does not know about, the task now tells you, so that work is not lost.
- A new task now gets its name much more often. Before, a task written as a question ("why does this break?") often stayed "Untitled". It also works now on computers where Claude was set up in a less usual way. And when Claude can't find a name, the bottom of the board says why.
- The three dots on task cards are gone. Click the card to open it, like before.
- The bar at the top no longer gets squashed when the window is short. Its buttons stay whole.
- In the list view, you can now drag the edge of the list of tasks to make it wider or narrower. Double-click the edge to put it back.
- The little tag on each card now has its own color for what Claude is doing: working, checking the work, writing the recap, merging, waiting for you, finished or gone. Writing the recap and merging now say so in words too.
- A task that is waiting for you now says so: it gets an orange edge, and finished work shows a "Waiting for you" tag until you answer, merge it or mark it done. The count at the top counts these too.
- In the list, one Create new task button at the top now makes a new task. The small + next to each group is gone. The new task always lands in To Do, and you see it there even while you search.
- In the list, the Filter button is gone. To find tasks, type in the search box at the top.
- In the list view, the list of tasks is now half as wide, so the open task gets more room. Long names get cut short with "…" instead of spilling out.
- When Claude asks you to pick, the choices are now buttons: click one to answer, or type your own. When Claude picks for itself, a check mark shows its choice.
- Turn on Notifications in the View menu to get normal pop-ups from your browser when Claude needs you or is done. Click one to open that card. With the board open in many tabs, you still get just one pop-up, and the board stays quick. The odd "Script Editor" pop-ups are gone.
- The top bar now shows which version the main copy of the app is really on, instead of always saying "main".
- You can now see all your tasks in one list, grouped by step, with the task you pick open right beside it, laid out like before: its choices on the left and the chat on the right. A Filter button picks which ones to show. The board view is still there in the View menu.
- When you send a task that looks like it belongs to another of your projects, the board now asks first. You can move it to that project and start it there, or keep it where it is.
- When Claude picks between options, its card now shows the choice as a small tree: what was asked, each option, which one was picked and who picked it.
- A card that only answers now gets a Create new task button when you ask it for work. The work goes to a new card that starts from this one's summary, so this card stays as it was.
- Making a new task from a finished card now works, even right after you finish it. Before, it showed an error while Claude was still writing the summary, or after it was done.
- Claude can no longer post a card message in another language than the one you wrote in. If you write in English, a message in French gets stopped, and Claude has to write it again in English.
- Claude's messages are easier to read: titles are bigger, there is more room between parts, and lists breathe. Tables, code boxes and quotes now show as real tables, boxes and quotes. Each review finding sits in its own card: a coloured tag for how serious it is, the title, then Where, Problem, Fix and Proof lined up, with the fix in its own box.
- When you press Send to Claude, the task page now switches to its summary at once. Claude gets started in the background while you look.

## 2026-10-14

- A card that went quiet now has a New task from this button. It writes a short summary of the work and makes a new card from it, so the next one starts light.
- When you press Send to Claude, the card now moves and shows it is sending right away. Its name and tags fill in a moment later, so it no longer looks stuck.
- If you write to Claude just as it finishes a task, the card now shows it working again while it does what you asked, instead of saying it is ready to merge.
- A task you leave without a name now gets a short name that says what it is about, written by Claude from what you wrote, instead of "Untitled".
- A new task with no tags now gets a few tags picked by Claude from what you wrote. It reuses the tags you already have, so you don't end up with both "bug" and "bugs", or "fix" and "fixes".
- A card no longer shows the Review code button once a review was already started, or once its work was merged. It comes back after you send a new message, but not when you only try the app.

## 2026-10-13

- When you scroll down a long column, like Done, it now stays where you left it instead of jumping back to the top a few seconds later.
- The buttons at the top (observations, unpushed work, update) now change by themselves right when Claude's work moves on, and catch up as soon as you come back to the window. No need to reload the page anymore.
- The Review code button no longer asks you to pick a budget. Every review now uses the same medium one, like it almost always did.
- Code reviews now also check that the work did what the card asked and follows the project's rules. A bug that was already there before is named in one line, and a second review says what changed since the first.
- Ticking "Answer only" now picks a quick, cheap helper for you: Haiku, or Luna when only Codex is set up. You can still pick another one.
- When a project has saved work that is not sent up to GitHub yet, an orange "↑ N unpushed" button shows at the top. Click it to see the list, and use "Push to remote…" there to send it up to the place you pick.

## 2026-10-11

- The Update button now also brings the helpers Claude works with. If you had an older board without them, they get set up on their own when the board starts again, and the button says "Setting up tools…" while it works.
- Under Tags, the tags you use most now show up on their own. Click one to add it to the task.
- Moving a card to Done now always gets it a summary. Before, if you moved it at just the wrong moment, the summary was skipped without a word.
- The Update button now puts in the new version even when you changed things in your copy, and keeps your changes. If the two don't fit together, it stops, changes nothing and tells you why.
- Renaming the Review or In Progress column no longer stops cards from moving there on their own.
- The board can now talk French. Open the View menu, pick Français under Language, and everything on the page changes. Pick English to go back.
- When a card's job is about another language, like translating into French, Claude now still answers you in the language you wrote in.
- When you tick "Answer only", the "Use a worktree" box now shows as off, because Claude won't use a side copy for it. Untick "Answer only" and your old choice comes back.
- When you fix or review the notes Claude keeps about its own skills, Claude now always works right in your project, never in a side copy.

## 2026-10-10

- The merge button now names the branch it merges into, like "Merge worktree to dev". Before, it always said "to main", even when the task was made from another branch and merged there.

## 2026-10-09

- Installing the board now also sets up the three helpers Claude works with: one that maps your code, one that remembers things between tasks, and one that keeps big outputs out of the way. Starting the board wakes up the memory helper.
- A task you send no longer stays on "Ready to send" while Claude is already working on it, even if you switch to another project while it starts.
- Each helper in the Subagents list now shows how much it holds in mind right now, the same number Claude Code shows, instead of a much bigger running total.
- Words you type in a task's message box but don't send now stay with that task. Other tasks show their own box, not yours.

## 2026-10-07

- Each card now shows how full Claude's memory of the chat is, like "14% ctx", and turns orange then red when it gets close to full. Open the card and look under "Context" to see how much it holds now, the most it held, and the most its model can hold.
- When you open the project list, a turning arrow shows next to each project where Claude is working right now.
- You can now pick who checks the work right next to the "Review code" button. When Codex checks Claude's work (or Claude checks Codex's), a "Send findings to Claude" button gives the list of problems back to the one who did the work, so it can fix them.
- Cards stop telling you about the weekly skill check. The Observations button now shows a "review due" mark when that check is due.
- Each card now shows how many tokens Claude used on it, its helpers included, under "Tokens used". Each helper also shows its own count. The big number only counts new words, not the old chat Claude reads again at every step.
- You can now pick an OpenAI model for a card, and Codex does the work instead of Claude. You can also pick who checks the work. When Codex splits the work between helpers, the card shows each helper and counts their tokens too. You can pick an OpenAI model for Codex's helpers too, like you can for Claude's.
- When Claude finishes and then adds a small note right after, the card now shows it as finished. Before, it said "stopped" even though the work was done.
- The Allow, Allow always and Deny buttons now work for long commands too, and when Claude asks about two things in a row. Before, the card just came back and Claude kept asking. If an answer still cannot be given, the card now says why. When Claude asks the very same thing twice, the card no longer says your answer failed: it tells you the second one is waiting. When Claude only offers yes or no, "Allow always" now says yes once and tells you so, instead of failing.

## 2026-10-06

- When Claude stops because its safety check keeps failing, the card now says so and the board gets Claude going again by itself after a minute. Before, the card just said "stopped" and waited for you.

## 2026-10-05

- When Claude asks if it may do something, you can now answer right on the card: Allow, Allow always or Deny. No need to open a terminal anymore.
- Helpers that Claude sent off and that already came back now show as finished. Before, they all looked "running" again each time you sent Claude a new message, even though they were doing nothing.

## 2026-10-04

- When Claude stops to ask if it may do something, the card now says "Claude asks permission" and shows what it wants to do (even right after Claude said it was done), with a button to copy the command that lets you answer. An old question you already answered no longer shows as a new one.

## 2026-10-02

- Claude no longer slips a task into a hidden copy of your project on its own. If your computer was set up that way, your changes seemed to vanish, the Run app button was missing, and the app started without them. Now only the board makes a copy, and only when the card's box is ticked.
- The "Use worktrees" box left the gear menu. Each card now has its own "Use a worktree" box, ticked for new cards. Untick it and Claude works right in your project folder. The choice is saved on the card, so an older window can no longer send a task the wrong way. If your folder has no git repo, the box that pops up now has a button to send the task without a worktree.
- The project list now drops down under the project name instead of covering it, so you can still see where you are.

## 2026-10-01

- The Subagents list is now short: each helper takes one line. Click a helper to see what it did last and the skills it used, and click again to fold it back. A helper that is still working also shows what it is doing now.
- When a task is split between helpers and you answer Claude's question, Claude now hands the new work to helpers again instead of doing it all alone.
- When a task is split between helpers, Claude now picks the right helpers' guides for each part and tells them to read those first, so each helper works the way the project wants.
- Each helper in the Subagents list now shows the skills it used, so you can see what each one is following.
- The "Next action" box now always shows "Merge worktree to main" and "Review code" side by side. You no longer have to read the changes or wait for a check before you can merge. To read the changes, open the Changes tab.
- When you press "Run app" on a website task, Claude now opens it in a new tab for you. Web addresses Claude writes on a card can now be clicked too, and open in a new tab.
- In the Changes tab, clicking an open change again now closes it. Before, it just opened the same thing again.
- A card in Done whose changes are not in your project yet now has a "Merge worktree to main" button in its "Next action" box. Before, the button never showed there.
- The board now notices when another window changed it (a card moved, a note typed) and shows the change within a few seconds, so you no longer need to reload the page. If you change something in an old window, the board reloads itself and asks you to do that change again, instead of putting the old board back. Sending a card to Claude from an old window does not start Claude; the board asks you to send again. The last change you make before closing the page is kept.
- While Claude is working, a small arrow turns in a circle next to "Work in progress" and on the card's "Claude: working" label, so you can see it is still busy and not stuck. It sits in the middle of the words and grows with the text size.
- When you send a message to a task that is done, it is not done anymore: it moves back to In Progress while Claude works on it.
- An open card has a new look. The left side shows the task's name, its column and priority, and a "Next action" box that tells you what to do now: wait, look at the changes, bring them in, or mark the task done. The big button there is the one to press. Under it, small fold-out rows hold the technical bits, the helpers Claude used and the words it was given.
- The right side of an open card now has four tabs: Summary (what Claude did, what changed and whether it is in your project yet), Changes (the files and saved changes, click one to see the difference), Conversation (all messages, with the reply box at the bottom) and Images. Finished tasks open on Summary, busy ones on Conversation.
- The "Next action" box looks at your project for real: it counts files that are changed and saved changes that are not in your main copy yet, so "nothing to merge" is only said when it is true. If it cannot look, it says so instead of showing zero.
- The orange note that a reply will cost more because Claude's memory went cold now sits right above the reply box and says how much it will reload.
- The row of buttons at the bottom of a card is gone. Deleting the task or its separate copy is under the "⋯" menu at the top right; the "Review code", "Run app" and "Merge" buttons moved into the Next action box and the Changes tab.
- The top bar is tidier: theme, text size and "Show subtasks" are under "View"; "Use worktrees" and the Changelog are under the gear button.

- When the board starts again, each card fills in as soon as it is ready, open cards first, instead of all of them staying empty until the very end.
- A small box at the top of the board shows which copy of the board you are using. It turns orange when you are on a task's separate copy instead of your main one, and you can pick another copy there to jump to it. The board will not throw away the copy it is running from: go back to main first. The top bar now stays on one line.
- While "Delete worktree" is working, the whole board turns grey and shows "Deleting the worktree…", so you cannot click anything by mistake until it is finished.
- If you write to Claude on a card after its separate copy was thrown away or brought into your project, Claude now gets a fresh separate copy to work in, so your own copy stays as it is.
- The "Claude finished" and "Claude needs you" pop-ups now wait until the card has moved or shows the change, so you no longer look at the board before it is ready.

If something goes wrong when bringing them in, Claude tells you on the card.
